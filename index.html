Babatunde Isaac Rotimi

Welcome — you have gradient descended into my local minima.

I'm a third-year PhD student in the Russell Seismology Group at Syracuse University, advised by Dr. Joshua Russell. My research focuses on developing novel algorithms that fuse multiple geophysical data types with physics-informed machine learning for subsurface imaging. I am trained in both active and passive source seismics, alongside other geophysical probing methods and high-performance computing, and I bring a strong combination of systems thinking, quantitative analysis, and hands-on experience integrating data, simulation, field operations, and scientific communication — with a focus on translating complex findings into actionable outcomes for diverse audiences.

I specialize in identifying the limitations of traditional geophysical workflows and deploying novel AI-driven approaches to push the boundaries of scientific inversion, signal processing, wave propagation, and generative AI. My goal is to bridge the gap between theory and practice, driving energy innovation for a sustainable future.

I'm also a Research Affiliate at Mineral-X, Stanford University, working with Dr. Peng Li on uncertainty quantification via physics-informed neural networks, and at the School of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences, Queensland University of Technology, Brisbane, Australia.

Before Syracuse, I completed my B.Sc. in Applied Geophysics at Adekunle Ajasin University in Nigeria, where I worked on seismic interpretation of 2D lines from the OPL 250 Deepwater, Niger Delta, and interned at Danvic Petroleum in Lagos. I hold a fellowship at the Geological Society of London, memberships in several professional organizations, and am part of LIGO — the world's largest scientific collaboration — where I dabble in gravitational wave observation and orbital mechanics.

When I'm not at a keyboard or out in the field, you'll find me hiking, I enjoy church service, giving back to my community, reading science fiction, trying (and failing) to keep houseplants alive, or losing at PS5 playing soccer with friends.
