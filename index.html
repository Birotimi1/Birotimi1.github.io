Current Focus

What I'm working on now

My PhD work centers on solving inverse problems with rigorous uncertainty quantification, fusing multimodal geophysical datasets, and bridging classical inversion with modern machine learning.

PhD Research

Hierarchical Bayesian Inversion Engine

Developing a novel hierarchical geophysical inversion framework that jointly inverts HVSR, Rayleigh wave ellipticity, and multimode surface-wave phase velocities for multi-scale crustal imaging — from soft sediments of the Atlantic Coastal Plain down through the lower Appalachians.

Inverse Problems Bayesian Methods Multimodal Fusion HVSR

Stanford · Mineral-X

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Subsurface Imaging

Research affiliate with Mineral-X at Stanford University, working on uncertainty quantification in subsurface imaging via physics-informed neural networks coupled with variational inference. Bridging data-driven and physics-based approaches.

PINNs Uncertainty Quantification Variational Inference PyTorch

Ongoing

Ambient Noise Tomography & Site Characterization

Characterizing near-surface shear-wave velocity structure of sedimentary basins to understand basin architectures related to continental breakup in the southeastern US. This improved imaging of soft sediments also contributes to ground motion modeling for seismic hazard assessment.

Ambient Noise Surface Waves Site Characterization DAS/Nodal

Background

Where I've been

Before Syracuse, I earned my B.Sc. in Applied Geophysics from Adekunle Ajasin University in Nigeria, where my thesis focused on seismic interpretation of 2D lines from deepwater Niger Delta. I then worked as a subsurface G&G intern at Danvic Petroleum in Lagos, conducting hydrocarbon evaluation through seismic interpretation and attribute analysis.

At Syracuse, I've worked across multiple projects — from 3D seismic interpretation of the Rockall Trough for hydrocarbon prospectivity and carbon sequestration under Dr. Christopher Scholz, to my current work developing hierarchical inversion methods with Dr. Joshua Russell. I've also completed field training with EarthScope at New Mexico Tech and broadband seismometer deployment at the LIGO Hanford site.

I'm a Fellow of the Geological Society of London and a member of AGU, SEG, AAPG, and NAPE. I also serve as a journal referee for SEG, Earth and Space Science, and G-Cubed.
