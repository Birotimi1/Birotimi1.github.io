PhD Research

Hierarchical Bayesian Inversion Engine

Developing a novel hierarchical geophysical inversion framework that jointly inverts HVSR, Rayleigh wave ellipticity, and multimode surface-wave phase velocities for multi-scale crustal imaging — from soft sediments of the Atlantic Coastal Plain down through the lower Appalachians.

Stanford · Mineral-X

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Subsurface Imaging

Research affiliate with Mineral-X at Stanford University under Dr. Peng Li, working on uncertainty quantification in subsurface imaging via physics-informed neural networks coupled with variational inference.

Ongoing

Ambient Noise Tomography & Site Characterization

Characterizing near-surface shear-wave velocity structure of sedimentary basins to understand basin architectures related to continental breakup in the southeastern US. Also contributing to ground motion modeling and shallow imaging via DAS/nodal arrays.

Background

Where I've been

Before Syracuse, I earned my B.Sc. in Applied Geophysics from Adekunle Ajasin University in Nigeria (Best Graduating Student), where my thesis focused on seismic interpretation of 2D lines from deepwater Niger Delta. I then worked as a subsurface G&G intern at Danvic Petroleum in Lagos.

At Syracuse, I've worked on 3D seismic interpretation of the Rockall Trough under Dr. Christopher Scholz, and now develop hierarchical inversion methods with Dr. Joshua Russell. I've completed field training with EarthScope at New Mexico Tech and broadband seismometer deployment at LIGO Hanford.

I'm a Fellow of the Geological Society of London and a member of AGU, SEG, AAPG, and NAPE. I serve as a journal referee for SEG, Earth & Space Science, and G-Cubed.
