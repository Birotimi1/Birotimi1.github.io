Babatunde Isaac Rotimi

Welcome — you have gradient descended into my local minima.

I'm a third-year PhD student in the Russell Seismology Group at Syracuse University, advised by Dr. Joshua Russell. My research focuses on developing novel methods for subsurface imaging that fuse multiple geophysical data types with physics-informed machine learning. I'm also a Research Affiliate at Mineral-X, Stanford University working with Dr. Peng Li on uncertainty quantification via physics-informed neural networks.

Before Syracuse, I completed my B.Sc. in Applied Geophysics at Adekunle Ajasin University in Nigeria (Best Graduating Student) and interned at Danvic Petroleum in Lagos. I'm a Fellow of the Geological Society of London and serve as a journal referee for SEG, Earth & Space Science, and G-Cubed.
