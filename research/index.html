Research

I use seismic waves to probe Earth's interior and understand the physical processes that shape our planet.

Seismic Imaging

Seismic Tomography & Subsurface Characterization

Developing and applying tomographic methods to map variations in seismic wave speed, revealing differences in temperature, composition, and melt/fluids. Includes regional-scale upper mantle studies and near-surface characterization.

TomographyInverse TheoryVelocity Models

Ambient Noise

Ambient Noise Seismology

Cross-correlating continuous seismic records to extract signals equivalent to earthquake data — even in regions with little seismicity. Powerful tools for imaging structure at scales inaccessible with traditional approaches.

Earth Structure

Lithosphere–Asthenosphere System

How seismic observations — wave speed, anisotropy, attenuation — constrain the rheological state of the upper mantle. Implications for mantle flow, plate evolution, and the role of volatiles and partial melt.

Mantle DynamicsAnisotropyAttenuation

Machine Learning

Physics-Informed Hybrid Learning

Bridging classical geophysical inversion with modern machine learning. Uncertainty quantification via physics-informed neural networks coupled with variational inference, in collaboration with Mineral-X at Stanford (Dr. Peng Li).

Gravity & Magnetic Methods

Gravity and magnetic surveys provide complementary constraints — delineating basin geometry, mapping geological contacts, and estimating sediment thickness where seismic data is limited.
