Research

I use seismic waves to probe Earth's interior and understand the physical processes that shape our planet's structure.

Seismic Imaging

Seismic Tomography & Subsurface Characterization

Seismic tomography allows us to create three-dimensional images of Earth's interior by analyzing how seismic waves travel through different materials. My work involves developing and applying tomographic methods to map variations in seismic wave speed, which reveal differences in temperature, composition, and the presence of melt or fluids in the subsurface.

This includes both regional-scale studies that image crustal and upper mantle structure, and more local investigations that characterize near-surface geology for engineering and environmental applications.

Ambient Noise

Ambient Noise Seismology

The Earth is constantly vibrating due to ocean waves, atmospheric pressure changes, and human activity. By cross-correlating continuous seismic records between pairs of stations, we can extract signals equivalent to those from earthquakes — even in regions with little seismicity. These ambient noise methods are powerful tools for imaging crustal and upper mantle structure at scales that are often inaccessible with traditional earthquake-based approaches.

Earth Structure

Lithosphere–Asthenosphere System

Understanding the mechanical and thermal structure of the lithosphere and underlying asthenosphere is fundamental to plate tectonics. I'm interested in how seismic observations — including wave speed, anisotropy, and attenuation — can constrain the rheological state of the upper mantle. This work has implications for understanding mantle flow patterns, the evolution of tectonic plates, and the role of volatiles and partial melt in weakening the asthenosphere.

Potential Fields

Gravity & Magnetic Methods

Gravity and magnetic surveys provide complementary constraints on subsurface structure by measuring lateral variations in density and magnetic susceptibility. These potential field methods are especially useful for delineating basin geometry, mapping geological contacts, and estimating sediment thickness in areas where seismic data is limited.
