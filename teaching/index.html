Teaching

I genuinely enjoy sharing knowledge with others and helping students discover the Earth sciences.

During my PhD at Syracuse University, I've had the opportunity to serve as an instructor for undergraduate lab and recitation sections — getting students hands-on with the fundamentals of how our planet works. I've TA'd for the following courses:

EAR 104 · Lab Instructor

Earth Science Laboratory

Labs covering Earth's interior, mineralogy, rock types, volcanoes and earthquakes, the geologic time scale, and radiometric dating.

EAR 105 · Recitation Instructor

Earth Science Recitation

Guided Learning Exploration and Geotours worksheet assignments — helping students connect lecture concepts to real-world geological features.

Graduate Coursework

Sedimentary Basin Analysis · Geophysics · Numerical Methods in Geosciences · Machine Learning in Earth Sciences · Geochemistry · Physical Hydrology · Plate Tectonics · Scripting for Data Analysis

I'm always open to new opportunities to teach, mentor, and learn from others. If you're looking for a guest speaker on geophysical imaging, inverse problems, or computational methods in the Earth sciences — reach out!
